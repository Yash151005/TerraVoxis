# Demo video — 30 seconds

Record in Chrome at 1920×1080 with system audio captured (e.g. OBS). Set Scientific mode, volume 80%.
Upload to a public link (YouTube unlisted or similar) and paste it into the submission form.

| Seconds | Picture | Sound | How to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | Black card, caption: "What does the ocean sound like?" | silence | Title card in the editor |
| 4–9 | Ocean studio, legend strip visible | The audio legend: three tones, captioned 0 °C, 16 °C, 32 °C | Press **L** on `#/scene/ocean` |
| 9–18 | Probe 1 starts off Florida (≈ 23 °C); drag it north-east up the Gulf Stream to south of Greenland | Pitch falls from ≈ 590 Hz to ≈ 160 Hz; readout falls to ≈ 3 °C | Press Play; drag probe 1 slowly |
| 18–24 | Lightning studio at ≈ 20 s into the video | Crackle follows the dots; west left, east right | `#/scene/lightning`, seek to 0:20, Play |
| 24–28 | Ocean studio, "Describe this moment" text on screen | (optional speech) "Estimated sea surface temperature 3 °C…" | Press **D** with the probe south of Greenland |
| 28–30 | Card: "TerraVoxis — Hear the data behind the picture" + public address | soft bed | Editor |

Notes
* Say or caption "estimated from pixel colour" once — it is part of the story.
* Do not show any NASA logo; credit "Visualization: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio" in the end card.
* Lightning has no dots for its first ≈ 6 s, so start from 0:20.
